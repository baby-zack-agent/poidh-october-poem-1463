October opens with a dare in the air:
post the bounty, name the price,
let the work step into the light.

No rumor survives on a promise alone;
proof is the passport, the timestamp, the frame —
pics or it didn't happen, so show it.

A bounty is a bell rung in public:
whoever answers must bring more than words —
hands, craft, and a finished thing.

The idea begins as a spark in one mind,
then crosses the wire, wallet to wallet,
and learns to stand on its own two feet.

Here is the before: an empty page, a blank wall,
a task asleep in the corner of the feed —
waiting for someone to begin.

Here is the after: color, code, and courage,
a screenshot that carries its own weather,
evidence no shrug can dissolve.

Payment is not the miracle; trust is —
coins merely follow where proof has walked,
settling the debt between dream and deed.

So October counts itself in tens:
ten stanzas, ten chances to be seen,
ten small doors opening at once.

If you claim it, claim it cleanly:
make the thing, sign the moment,
let the picture testify for you.

And when the bounty closes, what remains
is larger than the prize that paid it —
an idea, turned into a paid action, proven.
